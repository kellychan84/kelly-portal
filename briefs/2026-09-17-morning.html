🏦 联储三年来首次加息,鹰派信号点燃十月悬念 · The Fed's First Hike Since 2023
RATE HIKEFX168 · 已验证
联储全票通过加息25基点,沃什:通胀"太高、太久"
Fed unanimously hikes 25bp; Warsh says inflation "too high, for too long"
美联储于9月16日以12票全票通过,将联邦基金利率上调25个基点至3.75%-4.00%,这是自2023年7月以来的首次加息。主席沃什在记者会上直言:"通胀水平过高,而且持续时间过长",暗示这次加息不会是终点。更值得留意的是点阵图——16位官员认为2026年内还需再加息至少一次,其中4人预计全年累计加息达75个基点,利率中位数比6月预测整整上调30个基点(至4.1%)。市场原本期待一次"鸽派加息",但沃什明确表态不提供前瞻指引,让"温和收尾"的期待落空。反应立竿见影:黄金瞬间跳水近60美元,美元指数急拉约40点,10年期美债收益率一度触及5%这个自2007年以来的高位。
The Fed unanimously voted 12-0 to raise its target rate 25 basis points to 3.75%-4.00% on September 16 — the first hike since July 2023. Chair Kevin Warsh told reporters plainly that "inflation is too high and has been for too long," signaling this likely isn't the last move. More telling was the dot plot: 16 of 18 officials now expect at least one more hike this year, with four projecting a cumulative 75bp for 2026 — pushing the median rate forecast up 30bp to 4.1% from June's projection. Markets had hoped for a "dovish hike," but Warsh's refusal to offer forward guidance dashed that. The reaction was immediate: gold plunged nearly $60, the dollar index jumped about 40 points, and the 10-year Treasury yield briefly touched 5% — its highest since 2007.

Investing.com's Fed Rate Monitor (updated 7:15pm EDT, Sept 16) shows the October 28 meeting essentially a coin flip: 47.2% odds of holding at 3.75%-4.00% versus 52.8% for another hike to 4.00%-4.25%. December skews more hawkish — 48.5% probability of 4.00%-4.25% and 40.5% for 4.25%-4.50%, with only 11% odds rates stay unchanged. Traders are effectively pricing in that this hiking cycle isn't over. Adding fuel: August retail sales blew past forecasts (headline +1.2% vs +0.9% expected; ex-auto +1.4% vs +0.5% expected), giving the Fed more room — and reason — to keep tightening.

Malaysia holds its ground amid the global hiking storm: BNM steady, KLCI bucks the trend
当联储重启加息、全球市场神经紧绷的时候,大马这边反而呈现出一种"淡定"的对比。根据Trading Economics数据,国家银行(BNM)已连续七次货币政策会议维持隔夜政策利率(OPR)于2.75%不变,并表示"目前的政策立场仍适合维持物价稳定、支持经济增长"。数据也支持这个判断——今年上半年大马GDP增长达5.7%,主要受惠于出口表现强于预期与内需持续。通胀方面也相当温和,截至7月整体与核心通胀分别平均为1.8%和2%,即便全球大宗商品价格因中东局势走高,通胀压力仍属"可控"范围。另一边厢,富时隆综指(KLCI)在Investing.com最近一次可查证的收盘数据显示报1,698.01点,上涨11.27点(+0.67%)。这组数据是在联储决议前的市场情绪下录得的,综指对9月16日加息决定的完整反应仍待后续几个交易日观察,但至少目前为止,大马的基本面并没有因外围风暴明显动摇。
While the Fed reignites its hiking cycle and global markets grow jittery, Malaysia is showing a notably calmer picture. Trading Economics data shows Bank Negara Malaysia has held its Overnight Policy Rate steady at 2.75% for a seventh consecutive meeting, stating "the current policy stance remains appropriate for maintaining price stability and supporting economic growth." The numbers back that up: H1 2026 GDP grew 5.7%, driven by stronger-than-expected exports and resilient domestic demand. Inflation has stayed mild too — headline and core inflation averaged 1.8% and 2% respectively through July, described as "manageable" even with elevated global commodity costs from Middle East tensions. Separately, the most recently verifiable close for the FTSE Bursa Malaysia KLCI on Investing.com shows the index at 1,698.01, up 11.27 points (+0.67%). That data point was recorded ahead of the Fed's September 16 decision, so the index's full reaction is still playing out — but for now, Malaysia's fundamentals haven't been visibly rattled by the storm outside.

Micron Technology reports earnings on September 30, and Yahoo Finance frames it as the single most important catalyst for AI-memory stocks this year. Micron commands roughly 24% of the global DRAM market and 15% of NAND, making its results a bellwether for the entire memory sector. Wall Street expects about $50.8 billion in revenue this quarter — up 350% year-over-year — with non-GAAP EPS of $31.28, up 932%. Management's own guidance lines up with those numbers, underscoring confidence. The logic is straightforward: global AI data-center infrastructure spend is estimated at $750 billion this year and could top $1 trillion next year, and the resulting memory shortage has let Micron push prices sharply higher — gross margins have expanded from 39% to roughly 84.9%, with management guiding toward 86% next quarter. As the report puts it: "the insatiable demand for memory processors is not yet over."

今天这份简报有点像"三线作战"——联储加息、中东局势、AI股价分化,三条线同时在动,但仔细看会发现一个共同主题:市场正在从"情绪驱动"转向"甄别驱动"。加息不再单纯是"利空",而是要看有没有前瞻指引;财报不再是"达标就涨",而是要看背后的成本结构;油价不再是"越涨越慌",而是要看供需实质变化。对客户来说,这其实是提醒我们:比起追逐每一条headline,更值得投入时间的是回头检视自己的资产配置,是否真的经得起这种"细节决定涨跌"的市场环境。
Today's brief feels like three fronts opening at once — the Fed hike, Middle East tensions, and a widening split in AI stock reactions. But look closely and there's a common thread: markets are shifting from being sentiment-driven to being discernment-driven. A rate hike is no longer simply "bad news" — it now hinges on whether forward guidance comes with it. An earnings beat no longer guarantees a rally — investors are scrutinizing the cost structure behind it. Rising oil is no longer just "more panic" — it depends on whether the supply-demand picture has genuinely shifted. For clients, this is a good moment to look past the headlines and re-examine whether your own asset allocation can actually hold up in a market this detail-sensitive.
🎯退休规划与PRS
🎯 Retirement Planning & PRS
加息周期重启加上中东不确定性,是提醒大家检查PRS配置是否过度集中在单一资产类别——尤其如果距离退休还有10年以上,适度承受短期波动是合理的,但"适度"两个字很重要,建议每半年检视一次配置比例。
The renewed hiking cycle plus Middle East uncertainty is a good prompt to check whether your PRS allocation is too concentrated in one asset class. If retirement is still 10+ years away, some short-term volatility is reasonable to bear — but "reasonable" is the key word; a portfolio check every six months is worth the time.
💰DDA定期定额
💰 DDA (Dollar-Cost Averaging)
今天三条新闻线都指向同一个结论——短期方向真的很难预测(联储10月加不加息市场自己都还在47%对53%犹豫)。与其纠结"现在该不该进场",不如让DDA机制持续帮你在高低点之间自动分摊成本,把猜测的压力交给纪律。
All three stories today point to the same conclusion — even the market itself is split 47-53 on whether the Fed hikes again in October. Rather than agonizing over "should I invest now," let a DDA plan keep averaging your cost across highs and lows, and let discipline do the guessing for you.
📈长期投资
📈 Long-Term Investing
美光的毛利率从39%扩张到84.9%,这种规模的结构性改变,比任何一天的股价涨跌都更值得记在心上——它说明AI这个主题背后有真实的产业逻辑支撑,不是纯粹的情绪炒作。长期投资者该关注的是这类基本面变化,而不是被单日的红绿盘面牵着走。
Micron's gross margin expanding from 39% to 84.9% is a structural shift worth remembering far more than any single day's price move — it shows there's real industry logic behind the AI theme, not just sentiment. Long-term investors should anchor on fundamental shifts like this, not get pulled around by daily red-and-green swings.
