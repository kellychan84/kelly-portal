Hike odds climb to 93% as markets brace for a hawkish dot plot
Benzinga报道,联邦基金期货市场已将周三(马来西亚时间周四凌晨)加息25个基点的几率定价至93%,若落实将把利率推至3.75%–4.00%区间——是2023年以来美联储首次加息。分析师提醒,真正的看点不是"是否加息"(市场几乎已消化),而是沃什(Kevin Warsh)主席稍后公布的点阵图:如果显示年底前还有更多加息,今晚就不再是"一次性动作",而是"一轮紧缩周期的开端"。记者会预计在马来西亚时间周四凌晨2:30举行,通胀(核心PCE年底预估3.3%)、增长与失业率预测的调整都可能牵动市场。
Fed funds futures now price a 93% probability of a quarter-point hike, which would push rates to 3.75%–4.00% — the first increase since 2023, per Benzinga. The real story isn't the hike itself, which is largely priced in, but the dot plot: if officials signal more hikes before year-end, tonight stops being a one-off and becomes "the opening of a tightening campaign." Chair Warsh's press conference is set for 2:30am Thursday MYT, where updated inflation (core PCE ~3.3% by year-end), growth, and unemployment projections could move markets further.

10-year yield hits 5.04% — highest since 2007 — as tech names slide ahead of the Fed
据嘉信理财(Schwab)分析,美国10年期国债收益率一度触及5.04%,为2007年以来罕见高位。嘉信首席宏观研究员Kevin Gordon表示:"这些都在传递一个信号——通胀重返2%目标面临强大阻力,美联储因此越来越像是'必须加息'。"高企的收益率也拖累了美股:截至发稿,标普500下跌0.45%至7,600点,道指跌0.3%,纳指跌0.78%(Trading Economics数据),Alphabet、微软、甲骨文、亚马逊等重仓科技股均因借贷成本上升而走弱——这与今早简报中"AI安全警示压制芯片股"是同一枚硬币的两面:今天的压力来自利率,而非AI言论本身。
The 10-year Treasury yield touched 5.04% intraday — a rare level not seen since 2007, per Schwab. "All of that adds up to strong headwinds for inflation returning to 2%, and thus a Fed that is increasingly looking like it has to hike," said Kevin Gordon, Schwab's head of macro research. The elevated yield weighed on stocks: as of this check the S&P 500 is down 0.45% to 7,600, the Dow -0.3%, and the Nasdaq -0.78% (Trading Economics), with Alphabet, Microsoft, Oracle and Amazon all softer on rising borrowing costs — a second front, rates rather than AI-safety headlines, pressing the same tech names flagged this morning.

油价从四个月高点回落:美原油库存意外激增浇灭部分涨势
Oil retreats from 4-month highs as a surprise inventory build offsets Mideast risk
Trading Economics数据显示,截至发稿WTI原油报每桶104.21美元,单日下跌1.53%(-1.62美元);布伦特原油报107.68美元,下跌1.01%。这是对今早简报"油价飙破100美元"叙事的重要补充——美国原油库存上周意外激增714万桶,与市场原本预期的"去库存"相反,短期内压制了涨势。但地缘政治的支撑并未消失:沙特东西输油管道因无人机袭击仍处关闭状态(美国能源部长赖特称"数日内"可恢复);也门胡塞武装与沙特的冲突持续;利比亚两处油田及一处泵站也因抗议活动暂停运作。换句话说,油价涨势"踩了刹车",但引擎(供应风险)还在。
WTI is trading at $104.21/barrel, down 1.53% (-$1.62) on the day, while Brent sits at $107.68, down 1.01%, per Trading Economics. This tempers this morning's "oil breaks $100" story: US crude stockpiles surged by 7.14 million barrels last week — the opposite of the drawdown the market expected — unwinding some of the rally. But the underlying supply risk hasn't gone away: Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline remains shut after the drone attack (though US Energy Secretary Chris Wright says flows should resume "within days"), Houthi-Saudi fighting continues in Yemen, and Libya has suspended two oilfields and a pumping station amid protests. The rally paused; the risk premium didn't.

Everything we're seeing this evening is pre-decision positioning. Hike odds have firmed to 93%; what will actually move markets is the dot plot and Chair Warsh's press conference tone. The simple message for clients: no need to stay up for the result tonight, and no need to change a DCA plan or allocation over one meeting.
