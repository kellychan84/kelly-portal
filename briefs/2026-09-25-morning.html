On Thursday (Sept 24) the 10-year Treasury yield briefly touched 5.11%, its highest since 2007; the 2-year climbed to 4.897% (highest since 2023), and the 30-year hit a post-2004 peak of 5.438%. The driver isn't weakness — it's the opposite: S&P Global's business activity data showed the strongest growth in over five years. Capital.com's Daniela Hathorn put it plainly: "The economy is not merely avoiding recession; it appears to be accelerating." For bond markets, that means inflation and rate pressure may persist longer than expected. Mortgage rates have broken above 7.37%, the highest in over two years.

美伊探讨"分阶段"方案:伊朗重开海峡,美国解除封锁
US and Iran explore a "phased" framework: Iran reopens the strait, US lifts the blockade
路透社(经Investing.com转发,9/24发布)报道,美伊双方正探讨分阶段协议:伊朗恢复霍尔木兹海峡的自由通行,换取美国解除对伊朗的经济封锁,并可能释放被冻结的伊朗资产。核心障碍在于"谁先让步"的互信问题——伊朗希望制裁先松绑,华府则希望以施压换取实质让步,而8月一度达成又告吹的协议更加深了双方的疑虑。一位资深伊朗官员表示:"分阶段解决危机是一条可行之路,第一步是结束封锁、重开霍尔木兹。"前中东特使Dennis Ross评估,在11月3日中期选举前达成协议的机率约为三成。这与9月23日联合国大会期间美方特使Witkoff、Kushner、鲁比奥与伊朗外长Araghchi在卡塔尔、阿曼斡旋下的间接会谈相互呼应——伊朗官员称,若美方解除军事压力与封锁,海峡最快可在七天内重新开放。
Reuters, via Investing.com (published Sept 24), reports the US and Iran are discussing a phased deal: Iran restores free passage through the Strait of Hormuz in exchange for the US lifting its economic blockade and potentially unfreezing Iranian assets. The core obstacle is a trust problem over sequencing — Iran wants sanctions relief first, Washington wants concessions first, and a collapsed August agreement has deepened mutual suspicion. A senior Iranian official said: "One way forward would be to solve the crisis in stages. The first would be to end the blockade and reopen Hormuz." Former US Middle East envoy Dennis Ross puts the odds of a deal before the Nov 3 midterms at roughly 30%. This echoes the Sept 23 UNGA-sidelines talks where US envoys Witkoff, Kushner and Secretary Rubio met Iranian FM Araghchi via Qatari and Omani mediation — an Iranian official said the strait could reopen within seven days if US military pressure and the blockade ease.

"利好"消息下,油价为何不跌反涨?
If the news is "good," why did oil still climb?
照理说,重开海峡的谈判进展应压低油价,但周四WTI原油仍上涨1.63%收93.66美元/桶,布伦特原油涨1.53%至104.70美元。这恰恰说明市场的逻辑:在协议真正签署、海峡实际重新畅通之前,风险溢价不会消失,"谈判中的乐观"与"落地的确定性"是两回事。同一天,10年期殖利率触顶、经济数据偏热,也让部分资金转向大宗商品作为通胀对冲。对投资人而言,这是一堂生动的"消息与价格"课:利好新闻不等于价格立刻反映利好,市场永远在为"万一谈崩"的情境定价。
In theory, progress toward reopening the strait should push oil lower — but WTI still rose 1.63% Thursday to close at $93.66/barrel, with Brent up 1.53% to $104.70. That's exactly the market's logic at work: the risk premium doesn't disappear until a deal is actually signed and the strait genuinely reopens — "optimism in negotiation" and "certainty on the ground" are two different things. The same day's yield spike and hot economic data also pushed some capital toward commodities as an inflation hedge. It's a good real-time lesson: good news doesn't mean prices react immediately — markets are always pricing in the chance the deal falls apart.

特朗普盛情款待习近平,Altman、黄仁勋齐聚白宫国宴
Trump rolls out the red carpet for Xi, with Altman and Jensen Huang among the state dinner guests
周四(9/24)晚,白宫为到访的习近平举行国宴,OpenAI的Sam Altman、英伟达的黄仁勋、高通的Cristiano Amon、摩根大通的Jamie Dimon等科技与金融领袖悉数到场。特朗普形容:"所有人都想来,整个科技界、整个银行界,还有更多人都到齐了。"会谈中,习近平强调中美都是AI领域的领先者,呼吁"AI的发展应始终受人类掌控";美方则提出建立AI事故通报机制,以在涉及国家安全的AI事件上互相知会,但特朗普对具体监管路线仍语焉不详,一贯态度是抵制正式的AI限制。财政部长贝森特同时宣布中美贸易休战延长至明年1月10日(原定11月10日到期的"釜山协议")。不过据Al Jazeera的四大要点分析,伊朗议题在会谈中仍无突破——中国作为伊朗最大贸易伙伴,对孤立伊朗态度保留,习近平反而提议回到6月已破裂的临时协议框架;台湾问题上,习近平也向特朗普施压反对"台独"。
Thursday evening (Sept 24), the White House hosted a state dinner for visiting President Xi, with tech and finance leaders including OpenAI's Sam Altman, Nvidia's Jensen Huang, Qualcomm's Cristiano Amon, and JPMorgan's Jamie Dimon all in attendance. Trump described it: "Everybody wanted to be there. I would say you have the entire tech world, the entire banking world, and a lot more." During talks, Xi stressed both nations are AI leaders and called for "development of AI, always under human control"; the US proposed a notification system for AI incidents touching national security, though Trump's own regulatory stance stayed vague — he has consistently resisted formal AI restrictions. Treasury Secretary Bessent separately confirmed the US-China trade truce (the "Busan Agreement," originally set to expire Nov 10) is extended to January 10. Per Al Jazeera's four-takeaways analysis, however, the Iran issue saw no breakthrough — China, Iran's largest trading partner, remains reluctant to back isolating Tehran, with Xi instead proposing a return to the collapsed June interim framework; Xi also pressed Trump on opposing Taiwanese independence.

美光(Micron)将于9月30日公布第四财季业绩,市场预期每股盈余31.16美元,对比去年同期的3.03美元,几乎是十倍增长;营收预估约504亿美元(去年同期113.2亿美元)。驱动力是AI服务器对记忆体的需求持续超越供给,DRAM合约价格在今年首季飙涨90-95%,第二季58-63%,不过增速正在放缓,预期未来季度回落至13-18%。Benzinga追踪的27位分析师中,26位给予"买入"评级,平均目标价1,303美元,较现价(约974美元)仍有33.4%上行空间。风险在于估值——目前股价仅约6-8倍远期盈余,反映市场对"当前获利能否持续"仍存疑虑,记忆体产业历来周期性强,扩张期后往往伴随供过于求与价格回落。缓冲垫是美光已签下16份长期战略客户合约,涉及约1,000亿美元的未来履约金额,有助稳定订价。这份财报,某种程度上就是这一整年AI资本支出叙事的"期中考"。
Micron reports its fiscal Q4 on Sept 30. Wall Street expects EPS of $31.16, versus $3.03 a year ago — nearly a tenfold jump — on revenue of roughly $50.4 billion (vs. $11.32 billion a year ago). The driver is AI-server memory demand continuing to outpace supply: DRAM contract prices surged 90-95% in Q1 2026 and 58-63% in Q2, though the pace is cooling toward 13-18% quarterly gains ahead. Of the 27 analysts Benzinga tracks, 26 rate it Buy, with an average price target of $1,303 — 33.4% above the current ~$974 share price. The risk is valuation: the stock trades at just 6-8x forward earnings, reflecting skepticism that today's profit levels will hold, given memory's historically cyclical boom-bust pattern. The cushion: Micron has locked in 16 multi-year strategic customer contracts worth roughly $100 billion in future commitments. In a sense, this earnings report is the mid-year exam for the whole AI-capex narrative.
