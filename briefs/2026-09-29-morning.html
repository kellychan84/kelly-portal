美股三大指数集体收黑,10年期殖利率触及19年高点
US stocks close broadly lower as 10-year yield hits 19-year high
周一(9月28日),道琼斯工业指数收报51,481.51点,下跌347.11点(-0.67%);标普500报7,683.69点(-0.77%);那斯达克综合指数报26,820.38点(-0.92%)。三大指数同步走低,主因是10年期美债殖利率攀升至5.24%,创2007年以来新高,30年期殖利率更触及5.56%,为2004年以来最高。市场对通胀与利率维持高位的担忧持续升温。高盛策略师Ben Snider指出,标普500的远期本益比已从22倍降至19倍,回到十年均值水准——换句话说,估值的"泡沫"正随着利率上升被慢慢挤出。
Monday, Sept 28, the Dow closed at 51,481.51, down 347.11 points (-0.67%); the S&P 500 fell to 7,683.69 (-0.77%); the Nasdaq Composite dropped to 26,820.38 (-0.92%). All three indexes fell in tandem as the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.24%, its highest since 2007, while the 30-year hit 5.56%, a 2004 high. Concerns over persistently elevated inflation and rates kept building. Goldman Sachs strategist Ben Snider noted the S&P 500's forward P/E has "dropped to 19 times from 22 times," now back at its 10-year average — in other words, rising rates are slowly squeezing the valuation froth out of the market.
