也门胡塞武装弹道导弹瞄准利雅得，沙特七月来首次拉响空袭警报
Houthi ballistic missile targets Riyadh, first air-raid alert since July escalation
9月19日凌晨，伊朗支持的也门胡塞武装向沙特首都利雅得发射一枚弹道导弹，同时企图袭击比沙、塔伊夫、法拉桑及延布等多个城市，其中延布还波及沙特阿美石油设施。沙特联军发言人图尔基·马利基少将证实，防空系统成功拦截了这枚导弹，全国启动紧急警报，未造成人员伤亡或设施损坏——但利雅得王室国际机场附近仍可见到爆炸后的黑烟。这是自今年7月冲突升级以来，利雅得首次拉响空袭警报，联军已表态保留"适当"反击的权利。为什么重要？这提醒我们，即便石油价格近期连续三天回落，地缘政治的"安全边际"其实很薄——市场的平静，往往只是暂时的。
In the early hours of September 19, Iran-backed Houthi forces fired a ballistic missile at Riyadh while attempting to strike multiple Saudi cities — Bisha, Taif, Farasan, and Yanbu, the latter also affecting an Aramco facility. Coalition spokesman Major-General Turki al-Maliki confirmed Saudi air defenses intercepted the missile; the kingdom activated a nationwide alert with no casualties or damage reported, though a smoke plume was visible near King Khalid International Airport. This marks Riyadh's first air-raid alert since the conflict escalated in July, with coalition forces reserving the right to "appropriate" retaliation. Why it matters: even as oil prices have pulled back for three straight days, the geopolitical "margin of safety" remains thin — calm markets can be deceptively temporary.

Brent crude closed at $103.87/barrel on September 18, down 0.91% on the day and roughly 1.2% for the week — its third consecutive daily decline. AMD says AI adoption is "still early," sees no slowdown in demand
AMD股价9月18日盘前上涨0.95%，报550.25美元。首席技术官马克·佩珀马斯特（Mark Papermaster）表示，企业仍处于AI应用的早期阶段——虽然客户会更精挑细选工作负载以控制成本，但这并不会拖慢整体需求，公司也预期AI应用重心将从"训练"逐步转向"推理"与实际落地场景。他说："AI正在加速整个产业的一切"，并透露公司计划在2026年稍晚推出整机柜级AI解决方案，把硬件、软件与网络整合在一起。为什么重要？"需求仍在早期"这句话，是判断AI这波投资周期是否已经"透支"的重要风向标。
AMD shares rose 0.95% to $550.25 in Friday premarket trading. Why it matters: "still early" is the key phrase for judging whether this AI investment cycle has run ahead of itself or still has room to run.

🎯 退休规划与PRS
🎯 Retirement & PRS
本周的新闻提醒我们，退休规划最怕的不是波动本身，而是因为波动而做出情绪化的决定。无论是中东局势、油价还是联储政策，短期都无法预测，但PRS等长期配置工具的设计初衷，正是帮你穿越这些不确定性，专注在退休后的生活品质上。
This week's headlines are a reminder that the real danger in retirement planning isn't volatility itself — it's making emotional decisions because of it. Whether it's the Middle East, oil, or Fed policy, none of it is predictable in the short run. Tools like PRS exist precisely to help you look past that noise and stay focused on your quality of life after retirement.
💰 DDA定期定额
💰 DDA (Dollar-Cost Averaging)
从导弹新闻到AI财报倒数，这周的每一条headline，几乎都在测试投资人"忍不住想做点什么"的冲动。而定期定额的价值，恰恰在于它帮你把这种冲动变成一个不用天天纠结的自动化决定——涨的时候买一点，跌的时候也买一点，长期下来把情绪的成本降到最低。
From missile headlines to the AI-earnings countdown, this week tested every investor's urge to "do something." The value of dollar-cost averaging is exactly that it turns that urge into an automated decision you don't have to agonize over daily — buying a bit when it's up, a bit when it's down, and letting time reduce the emotional cost of investing.

📈 Long-Term Investing

AI infrastructure capex commitments — like Broadcom's guidance from $115B to $230B — span two to three years, exactly the kind of higher-visibility growth story long-term investors should watch. Likewise, short-term pressure on Malaysian markets shouldn't be read as the end of long-term opportunity — what matters is whether your portfolio has enough global diversification to balance both local and international cycles.
