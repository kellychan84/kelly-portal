巴菲特卸任伯克希尔董事长,"时间永远是赢家"
At 96, Buffett Steps Down as Berkshire Chairman: "Father Time Always Wins"
96岁的股神沃伦·巴菲特于9月18日正式卸下执掌逾五十年的伯克希尔·哈撒韦董事长职位,转任"名誉董事长"并继续留任董事会。他引用了那句广为流传的话:"时间永远是赢家,不过它对我已经很仁慈了。"这不是一次仓促的退场——去年他已先卸任CEO交棒给Greg Abel,这次交出的是公司文化与治理的最后一根接力棒。对普通投资者而言,这提醒我们:再伟大的投资旅程,最终考验的不是谁在场上,而是有没有把"传承的规则"提前写好。
Warren Buffett, 96, stepped down as Berkshire Hathaway's chairman on September 18 after more than five decades at the helm, moving to the board as "chairman emeritus." He reflected: "Father Time always wins. He has, however, been generous with me." This caps a transition that began months earlier when he ceded the CEO role to Greg Abel — the final handoff wasn't about capital, but about who protects the culture next.

Goldman Sachs: Not a Bubble, Just an "Orderly Cyclical Peak" — But Watch the Circular Financing
高盛策略师Ben Snider明确反驳"AI泡沫论",称目前更像是一次"有序的周期性顶峰",而非崩盘前兆。他预测标普500企业盈利增速将从今年二季度的51%大幅放缓至2027、2028年的11%(低于市场普遍预期的19%和17%)。目前支撑盈利的三大"临时性"因素包括:超大规模云厂商今年合计约8000亿美元的资本开支、记忆体芯片毛利率逼近80%(历史均值约55%)、以及科技公司靠私募投资入账的1500亿美元非经营性收入。高盛也罕见提出警讯:云厂商、芯片商、AI开发商之间"互相输血"的循环融资模式,存在传导风险。对客户而言这不是"要不要退出AI主题"的问题,而是"手上的仓位够不够分散"的问题。
Goldman strategist Ben Snider rejected the AI-bubble narrative, calling current conditions an "orderly cyclical peak" rather than a pre-crash setup. He projects S&P 500 earnings growth will decelerate from 51% in Q2 to just 11% in both 2027 and 2028 — below consensus of 19% and 17%. Three temporary tailwinds are propping up earnings: ~$800B in 2026 hyperscaler capex, memory-chip margins near 80% (vs. a historical ~55%), and $150B+ in non-operating income tech firms are booking from private investments. Goldman flagged a real risk though — "circular financing" among hyperscalers, chipmakers, and AI developers funding each other, which could transmit shocks across the sector.

The ringgit held at 4.0973 per USD, down a modest 0.02% daily but 0.95% weaker over the month and 2.62% year-over-year. Continued foreign profit-taking amid the Fed's unwavering hawkish stance remains a key driver of the pressure on local equities. For clients running disciplined PRS or DCA plans, this kind of monthly-scale volatility is exactly the textbook case for staying the course rather than trying to time a bottom.
