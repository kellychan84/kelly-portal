Micron's Sept 30 print and the US-China AI-hotline talks are two sides of the same story: one is the real-world payoff of AI infrastructure demand (memory shortages, rising prices), the other is the "rulebook" for this industry being slowly negotiated between two great powers. The former drives short-term share-price swings; the latter determines how far and how steadily the sector can run. Both deserve a place in long-term allocation thinking — just size the weight sensibly, and don't put all your eggs in one basket over a single bright earnings print.
