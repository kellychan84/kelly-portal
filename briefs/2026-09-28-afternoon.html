Hike odds climb to 73.5% by afternoon, well above this morning's 66.6%
今早简报时,Investing.com的Fed Rate Monitor显示10月28日FOMC加息机率为66.6%;截至本次更新,这个数字已经走高至73.5%,维持不变的机率则从33.4%降到26.5%。数字本身没有戏剧性的新闻触发,更像是市场在消化一整天累积的信号——油价走高、殖利率同步攀升,都在悄悄把"联储还得继续压通胀"这个判断说得更肯定。对我们来说,与其追着每一次机率数字的跳动,不如记住:利率环境高位震荡,本来就是这几个月的常态,不是意外。
At this morning's brief, Investing.com's Fed Rate Monitor showed a 66.6% probability of an October 28 hike; by this update that's climbed to 73.5%, with hold odds slipping from 33.4% to 26.5%. There's no single dramatic headline behind the move — it reads more like the market digesting a full day of signals, with oil and yields both drifting higher reinforcing the "the Fed still has work to do" read. The takeaway for us isn't to chase every tick in the probability — it's to remember that a choppy, elevated-rate backdrop has simply been this quarter's normal.

Kelly's Takeaways · 午间总结(顾问分析,非新闻引述)
今早我们才刚聊过"美股周涨、油价反弹、联储机率66.6%"这三件事,才过了短短一个下午,同样的三条线全部又往同一个方向走了一步——加息机率跳到73.5%,油价再涨近2%,殖利率飙向2004年以来高点。没有一个是突发的大新闻,更像是市场在同一份剧本上,把音量又调大了一格。这种"没有新剧情、却持续升温"的状态,其实才是最考验投资纪律的时候——因为它不会给你一个明确的"现在该做什么"的信号,只会让人忍不住想做点什么。而多数时候,按兵不动、维持既定配置,才是对的答案。
This morning we talked about three threads — US stocks' weekly gain, oil's rebound, and the Fed sitting at 66.6% — and in the span of one afternoon, all three moved another notch in the same direction: hike odds to 73.5%, oil up another ~2%, yields pushing toward a 2004-era high. None of it is a single dramatic headline — it's more like the market turning up the volume on the same script. This kind of "nothing new, just intensifying" state is actually the hardest test of discipline, because it never hands you a clear "do something now" signal — it just tempts you to act anyway. Most of the time, staying put with your existing allocation is still the right call.
