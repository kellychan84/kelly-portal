佩泽希齐扬联大发言强硬,美方代表中途离场
Pezeshkian's defiant UN address prompts US walkout
伊朗总统佩泽希齐扬周三(9/23)在联合国大会上罕见强硬发声,直指"是我们才是恐怖主义的受害者",并展示据称是米纳卜(Minab)一所女子学校遭空袭遇难学生的照片,强调伊朗"从未低头、从未屈膝"。他同时表示愿意伸出双手寻求区域合作,但强调这是出于"对自身力量与坚韧的深切信念",而非软弱。美方一名代表在演说过程中中途离席,以色列代表团则全程缺席。这场演说,冲淡了本周稍早库什纳(Kushner)与威特科夫(Witkoff)团队与伊朗官员"非常好"的三小时会谈所带来的乐观情绪。
Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian delivered a defiant UN General Assembly speech Wednesday (Sept 23), countering Trump's "terrorist" framing by saying "we are the victims of terrorists" and displaying photos of schoolgirls he said were killed in an airstrike on a school in Minab. He said Iran "did not bow our heads or bend the knee," while still extending "hands towards the world" for regional cooperation — framed from strength, not weakness. A US delegation official walked out mid-speech, and Israeli representatives boycotted entirely. The address undercut the cautious optimism from Tuesday's "very good," three-hour Kushner-Witkoff talks with Iranian officials.

Oil snaps back sharply, WTI clears $94
受地缘政治紧张情绪升温带动,WTI原油周四(9/24)上涨1.95美元(+2.11%)至94.11美元,布伦特原油上涨2.87美元(+2.78%)至105.95美元,一举收复了周三受外交乐观情绪带动的跌幅。这也呼应了周三"伊朗总统在联大誓言绝不投降"带动油价止跌回升近2%的走势。市场对霍尔木兹海峡局势缓解的乐观预期,再次因外交进展的不确定性而降温。
WTI crude jumped $1.95 (+2.11%) to $94.11 Thursday, while Brent gained $2.87 (+2.78%) to $105.95, reversing Wednesday's dip that had followed diplomatic optimism from the Kushner-Witkoff talks. The move echoes Wednesday's near-2% oil rebound after Iran's president vowed at the UN his country would "never surrender." Optimism about a Hormuz reopening is cooling again as the diplomatic picture turns murkier.

财长贝森特宣布中美贸易休战延长至明年1月10日
Bessent confirms US-China trade truce extended to Jan 10, 2027
习近平周四抵达美国,与特朗普会面之际,美国财长贝森特宣布,中美"釜山协议"(Busan Agreement)贸易休战将延长至2027年1月10日——比原定11月10日到期日多出约两个月。市场原本被通胀与美债收益率飙升的忧虑笼罩(周四早盘标普期货跌0.4%、道指期货跌0.2%、纳指100期货跌0.5%),这则消息为整体情绪带来一丝喘息空间,不过尚未完全抵消债市带来的压力。
As Xi Jinping landed in the US Thursday ahead of his meeting with Trump, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced the "Busan Agreement" trade truce would be extended through January 10, 2027 — roughly two months beyond its original November 10 expiration. The news offered some relief against a backdrop of inflation anxiety and surging Treasury yields (S&P futures -0.4%, Dow futures -0.2%, Nasdaq-100 futures -0.5% Thursday morning), though it hasn't fully offset the bond-market pressure.
