Yields hit a near-20-year high — the market's "floor" and "ceiling" are both in play
周五(9/25)收盘,10年期美债殖利率一度冲上5.225%,是2007年金融危机以来最高;30年期更触及5.502%,创2004年以来新高。标普500、道琼斯、纳指三大指数当天仍靠科技巨头撑起涨势,分别收在7,743.41(+0.51%)、51,828.62(+0.93%)、27,068.72(+0.48%),标普与纳指本周分别累计上涨0.63%与1.21%。有分析师把这种矛盾局面总结得很精辟:"企业盈利能力是撑住市场的地板,而地缘政治与政策不确定性,则是压住市场的天花板。"贝莱德固定收益主管Rick Rieder的评论也值得记下来:"这不是一场危机,而是一记警钟。"换句话说,市场还没有慌,但已经开始留意。
At Friday's (Sept 25) close, the 10-year Treasury yield briefly touched 5.225% — the highest since the 2007 financial crisis — while the 30-year hit 5.502%, a post-2004 high. Yet the S&P 500, Dow, and Nasdaq still closed up on megacap tech strength, at 7,743.41 (+0.51%), 51,828.62 (+0.93%), and 27,068.72 (+0.48%) respectively, with the S&P and Nasdaq up 0.63% and 1.21% for the week. One analyst summed up the tension well: "Corporate profitability is providing the floor supporting the market while geopolitical and policy uncertainty are creating a ceiling." BlackRock's Rick Rieder put it memorably too: "Not a crisis but an eye-opener." In short — the market isn't panicking, but it's paying closer attention.

Kelly's Takeaways · This Week's Summary (advisor analysis, not news reporting)
这周最有意思的地方,是"过热"这个词同时出现在两个完全不同的语境里:债市因为经济数据太强而过热,消费者却因为油价与关税太贵而喊累。华尔街和寻常家庭,看的是同一个经济体,感受到的却是两种完全不同的温度。与此同时,霍尔木兹的"7天方案"与特习会的"AI热线",都是"进展存在,但远未落地"的典型——世界正在谈,但还没有谈成。对我们做规划的人来说,这种"半成品"式的新闻周,恰恰最考验纪律:不因为半个好消息就冲动加仓,也不因为半个坏消息就恐慌离场。
The most interesting thing this week is that "overheating" showed up in two completely different contexts at once: bonds overheated because the economy is running too strong, while consumers are tired because gas and tariffs are running too expensive. Wall Street and ordinary households are looking at the same economy but feeling two very different temperatures. Meanwhile, both Hormuz's "7-day plan" and the Trump-Xi "AI hotline" are classic cases of "progress exists, but nothing is finished" — the world is talking, but hasn't closed the deal. For those of us doing planning, a week full of half-finished stories is exactly what tests discipline the most: don't chase in on half-good news, and don't panic-exit on half-bad news either.

💰 DDA定期定额
💰 DCA (定期定额)
这周的三条线——债市、油价、外交谈判——没有一条真正"结束",每一条都还在半路上。这正是定期定额存在的意义:您不需要预判哪条线先落地,只需要按计划继续参与市场,让时间替您做筛选。
None of this week's three storylines — bonds, oil, diplomacy — actually concluded; each is still midway through. That's exactly why dollar-cost averaging exists: you don't need to predict which one resolves first, just keep showing up on schedule and let time do the sorting for you.
📈 长期投资
📈 Long-Term Investing
"企业盈利是地板,地缘政治是天花板"——这句话值得记一整年。只要盈利这个地板还在,短期的天花板波动就不该改变长期配置的骨架。真正的长期投资者,关心的是美光十年后还站不站得住,而不是这周股价涨跌几个百分点。
"Corporate earnings are the floor, geopolitics is the ceiling" — a line worth remembering all year. As long as that floor holds, short-term ceiling volatility shouldn't reshape the skeleton of a long-term allocation. A real long-term investor cares whether Micron still stands a decade from now, not how many percentage points the stock moved this week.
