川普周四将与习近平会晤,科技巨头CEO齐聚晚宴
Trump-Xi summit set for Thursday, tech CEOs to attend state dinner
川普总统将于本周四与中国国家主席习近平举行高层会晤,议题聚焦关税暂停协议的延期,以及人工智能领域的合作。据报道,包括英伟达黄仁勋、OpenAI的Sam Altman、高通的Cristiano Amon、微软的Satya Nadella以及苹果的Tim Cook等科技领袖将出席与习近平的晚宴。美国财政部长贝森特(Scott Bessent)形容初步的中美会谈"非常成功",并提议建立中美人工智能对话机制。市场情绪因此转趋乐观——道指期货、标普期货齐涨0.5%,纳指期货涨0.8%,道指此前刚经历连续三周下跌。分析指出,尽管股市今年"以顽强姿态穿越了担忧的高墙",但中东地缘风险、债券收益率攀升与AI估值疑虑仍是投资者需要留意的逆风。
President Trump is set to meet Chinese President Xi Jinping this Thursday, with talks expected to focus on extending the tariff truce and deepening AI cooperation. Tech leaders including Nvidia's Jensen Huang, OpenAI's Sam Altman, Qualcomm's Cristiano Amon, Microsoft's Satya Nadella, and Apple's Tim Cook are reportedly set to attend a dinner with Xi. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent called preliminary US-China talks "very successful" and proposed a formal US-China AI dialogue. Sentiment turned modestly optimistic on the news — Dow and S&P futures both rose 0.5%, Nasdaq futures +0.8%, after the Dow's third straight weekly loss. Analysts note stocks have "powered through a wall of worry" this month, though Middle East risk, rising yields, and AI valuation jitters remain headwinds to watch.

伊朗经卡塔尔转交六项停战条件,川普提前结束度假返回华府
Iran sends six war-ending conditions via Qatar; Trump cuts Camp David trip short
伊朗安全高层拉扎伊(Mohsen Rezaei)透过卡塔尔斡旋,向美方转交六项停战条件:全面停止包括黎巴嫩战线在内的战事、解冻数十亿美元遭冻结资金、终止对伊朗港口的海上封锁、停止干涉伊朗内政、停止支持"反对伊朗革命"的势力,以及停止对伊朗领土的一切攻击。伊朗国会议长加利巴夫(Ghalibaf)表态,霍尔木兹海峡将维持封锁,直至上述条件获得满足。川普总统对此尚未正面回应,但美国已对九个中东国家发出旅游警示,川普本人也提前结束原定于戴维营的周末行程,于周六无预警返回华府。分析人士认为,川普可能希望在11月中期选举前促成协议,但双方立场差距仍大,部分观察者甚至认为伊朗的条件更像是"不切实际的谈判立场",而非认真的和谈提议。
Iran's security chief Mohsen Rezaei relayed six conditions for ending the war to the US via Qatari mediators: a full ceasefire including on the Lebanon front, release of billions in frozen Iranian funds, an end to the naval blockade on Iranian ports, a halt to US interference in Iran's internal affairs, an end to US support for "opponents of the revolution," and a stop to all US attacks on Iranian territory. Parliament Speaker Ghalibaf said the Strait of Hormuz will stay closed until these demands are met. Trump has not directly responded, though the US issued travel warnings for citizens in nine Middle East nations, and Trump himself cut his Camp David weekend short, returning to Washington on Saturday without explanation. Analysts suggest Trump may be angling for a deal before November's midterms, though the gap between the two sides remains wide — some view Iran's list as an unrealistic opening position rather than a serious offer.

油价续跌至98美元下方,联储10月加息概率同步降温
Oil slips below $98 as Fed's October hike odds ease in tandem
受惠于市场对美伊可能恢复外交对话的期待,WTI原油周一下跌2.13%至每桶98.16美元,布伦特原油跌1.97%至101.83美元——这是近期第五个交易日走跌。与此同时,联储10月28日议息会议的加息概率(升至4.00%-4.25%)已从今早的59.7%回落至55.1%,维持不变的概率则从40.3%回升至44.9%,据Investing.com利率监测工具。油价降温有助缓解通胀压力,这也是市场重新调低加息预期的部分原因——两者呈现同步松动的态势,但需注意联储路径仍以"倾向加息"为主流情景,尚未出现方向性反转。
Oil extended its pullback as markets grew hopeful that US-Iran diplomatic talks could resume — WTI fell 2.13% to $98.16/barrel and Brent slid 1.97% to $101.83 on Monday, a fifth straight session of declines. In parallel, per Investing.com's Fed Rate Monitor, the odds of an October 28 hike to 4.00%-4.25% eased to 55.1% from 59.7% this morning, while hold-odds rose to 44.9% from 40.3%. Cooling oil eases inflation pressure, which is part of why hike odds are softening too — a modest, tandem loosening, though a hike still remains the base case rather than a reversal in direction.
