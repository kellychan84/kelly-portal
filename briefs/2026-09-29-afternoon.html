伊朗外长阿拉格齐周一在纽约与卡塔尔调解人会面,讨论重开霍尔木兹海峡的方案,随后即离开纽约,表示"卡塔尔知道如何联系我"。一名不具名美方官员向半岛电视台表示,谈判"积极且具建设性",伊朗在核问题上也展现了一定弹性,但双方在承诺的时序与先后顺序上仍有分歧。与此同时,CBS News报道特朗普在Truth Social上否认了外界关于他曾提出解除制裁、解冻伊朗资产的说法,直言"我什么都没给他们"。伊朗最高领袖办公室及军方仍维持强硬表态,称霍尔木兹海峡依旧封闭。整体来看,这不是突破,但语气比上周末明显缓和了一些。
Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met Qatari mediators in New York on Monday to discuss reopening the Strait of Hormuz, then left the city saying "Qatar knows how to reach me." An unnamed US official told Al Jazeera the talks were "positive and constructive," noting Iran has shown some flexibility on nuclear matters, though the two sides remain divided on the timing and sequencing of commitments. Separately, CBS News reported Trump denied on Truth Social that he'd offered Iran sanctions relief or access to frozen assets, saying plainly "I offered them NOTHING!" Iran's supreme leadership and military still maintain a hard line, insisting the Strait remains closed. Nothing here is a breakthrough, but the tone has clearly softened from the weekend's rejection.

This morning we talked about three things — stocks pulling back, the Fed at 72.3%, and oil cooling slightly off Monday's high. In the span of one afternoon, the picture got a little more nuanced: the Fed's odds kept climbing to 74.6%, but oil — unusually — didn't follow, easing back instead; and more notably, the previously tense Iran standoff produced its first "positive and constructive" language today. My reminder for you: this doesn't mean the risk has cleared, and it's not a cue to chase the news either direction — it's simply proof that the situation keeps shifting, and our response was never meant to dance to every headline's mood, but to hold the existing allocation rhythm and let time and discipline do their work.
