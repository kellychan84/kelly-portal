🚀 美光财报与AI存储 · Micron and the AI Memory Boom
BLOWOUTCNBC · 已验证 Verified
美光交卷:营收近乎翻两番,AI存储需求依旧强劲
Micron delivers: revenue nearly quadruples as AI memory demand stays red-hot
美光公布2026财年第四季度业绩:经调整每股盈余33.42美元(预期31.61美元),营收542.3亿美元(预期510.7亿美元),约为去年同期113.2亿美元的近四倍。数据中心业务营收超过250亿美元;数据中心固态硬盘(SSD)营收近100亿美元,是去年同期的10倍以上。公司预计下一季度营收约615亿美元、经调整每股盈余38.15美元。盘后股价小幅上扬。为什么重要:这是检验"AI基础设施需求"最直接的一份财报,但股价已在一年内大涨逾500%,好消息很大程度上已反映在价格里。
Micron reported fiscal Q4 2026 adjusted EPS of $33.42 (vs $31.61 expected) and revenue of $54.23B (vs $51.07B), almost four times the $11.32B of a year earlier. Data center revenue topped $25B, and data center SSD revenue was nearly $10B, more than 10x the year-ago quarter. It guided next-quarter revenue to about $61.5B and adjusted EPS to $38.15. Shares rose slightly in extended trading. Why it matters: this is the most direct test of AI-infrastructure demand — but the stock is already up more than 500% in a year, so much of the good news is priced in.

PCE inflation undershoots, but markets are not out of the woods
美国8月个人消费支出(PCE)物价指数按月升0.3%,年增3.4%,低于经济学家预期的3.7%;剔除食品与能源的核心PCE月增0.2%,年增3.0%,同样低于预期。CNBC的分析标题直言:通胀走向正确方向,但市场"仍未脱离险境"。
The PCE price index rose 0.3% on the month and 3.4% year on year, below the 3.7% economists expected; core PCE (ex food and energy) rose 0.2% m/m and 3.0% y/y, also below forecasts. CNBC's analysis headline: inflation is moving in the right direction, but markets are "still not out of the woods."
