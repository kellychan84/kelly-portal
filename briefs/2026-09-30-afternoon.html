Hike odds slip another 2.3 points as the market tilts toward a hold
截至9月30日凌晨2:55(美东时间),Investing.com的Fed Rate Monitor显示,市场对10月28日议息会议维持3.75%-4.00%的概率已升至52.9%,加息25个基点的概率则降至47.1%——比今早早报所引用的49.4%又低了一截。距离周一的74.6%,加息概率在短短两天内几乎腰斩。这不是逆转,而是同一条"消费者信心探底"叙事的延续:数据越弱,市场就越倾向于相信联储局会按兵不动。
As of 2:55am EDT on Sept 30, Investing.com's Fed Rate Monitor shows the odds of holding at 3.75%-4.00% at the Oct 28 meeting have risen to 52.9%, with hike odds down to 47.1% — a touch lower than the 49.4% cited in this morning's brief. That's down from 74.6% just two days ago on Monday. This isn't a reversal — it's the same "consumer confidence cracking" story continuing to play out: the weaker the data looks, the more the market leans toward a Fed that stands pat.

SANCTIONSCBS News · 已验证 Verified
美国财政部加码制裁,谈判桌外的另一手
Treasury adds sanctions as talks continue on a separate track
美国财政部于9月29日晚间宣布,对10名个人及企业实施制裁,指控其协助向伊朗军方后勤机构供应武器与电子设备,行动隶属"经济弃儿行动"(Operation Economic Outcast)。财政部长贝森特(Scott Bessent)表示:"财政部不会容忍任何对该政权的支持,并将持续识别、揭露、孤立伊朗的协助者。"值得留意的是,这与卡塔尔斡旋下、围绕霍尔木兹海峡的间接谈判是两条并行的轨道——经济施压并未因谈判仍在进行而暂停,反而提醒市场,油价的"降温"背后,地缘局势远未真正解决。
On the evening of Sept 29, the US Treasury announced sanctions on 10 individuals and entities accused of helping supply weapons and electronics to Iran's military logistics agency, under "Operation Economic Outcast." Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said: "Treasury will not tolerate any support to the regime and will continue to identify, expose, and isolate Iran's enablers." Worth noting: this runs on a separate track from the Qatar-mediated indirect talks over the Strait of Hormuz — economic pressure hasn't paused just because talks continue, a reminder that oil's recent cooling doesn't mean the standoff is actually resolved.

Micron reports fiscal Q4 2026 results after the US market close. Per Yahoo Finance's compiled analyst estimates: revenue is expected to hit $51.24 billion, up 353% year-on-year — "the highest growth on record" — while adjusted EPS is projected to jump 943% to $31.59. The consensus price target is $1,515.54, roughly 40% above the last close, with Wells Fargo at $1,525 and Citi at $1,300. Beyond the headline numbers, the market is watching closely for a possible fresh buyback announcement, which could be an added catalyst. Micron shares are already up 279% year-to-date and 13% in the month heading into earnings — expectations are running high, which also means "priced for perfection" risk can't be fully ruled out.

Kelly's Takeaways · 午后总结(顾问分析,非新闻引述)
今早的三条线——信心探底、AI公约、美光倒数——到了午后基本延续了原来的方向,没有出现逆转,但每一条都多了一点"细节"。联储局的加息概率继续往下走,伊朗那边则是制裁和谈判同步进行,美光的悬念也进入了最后几个小时。今晚真正值得留意的,反而是马来西亚这条线上出现的数据不一致——它提醒我们,"看新闻"和"看对新闻"是两回事,尤其在信息量大的日子。
The three threads from this morning — cracking confidence, the AI accord, Micron's countdown — mostly held their direction into the afternoon, with no reversal, but each picked up a bit more detail. Fed hike odds kept drifting lower, sanctions and negotiations with Iran are running on parallel tracks, and Micron's suspense is now down to its final hours. What's actually worth noting tonight is the data inconsistency on Malaysia — a reminder that reading the news and reading it correctly aren't always the same thing, especially on a data-heavy day.
利率与地缘这两条线,今天更像是"确认"而不是"转折"：加息概率从74.6%一路松动到47.1%,方向没变,只是又往前走了一步;伊朗的制裁与谈判同时进行,也说明这类僵局往往不是"非黑即白"地解决,而是拖着走。对客户而言,这两条线合起来讲的是同一件事——不确定性本身,就是需要被规划进组合里的常态,而不是等它"结束"才开始配置。
The rate and geopolitical threads today read more like confirmation than a turning point: hike odds kept easing from 74.6% toward 47.1%, same direction, just another step further; and sanctions running alongside talks with Iran show how these standoffs rarely resolve cleanly — they tend to drag on. Put together, they're telling clients the same thing: uncertainty itself is the steady state to plan a portfolio around, not something to wait out before allocating.
