我们收集哪些资料

当你使用退休计算器或预约咨询时，我们会收集你主动提供的姓名、手机号码、电邮，以及你在表格中填写的资料（例如年龄、储蓄金额、想讨论的主题）。

资料保存与分享

你的资料储存在 Kelly Chan 私人管理的 Google 账户中，不会出售或出租给任何第三方。仅在法律要求或为提供你所要求的服务而必要时，才会与相关机构分享。

你的权利

你可随时要求查阅、更正或删除你的个人资料，或要求停止接收任何资讯。请通过 WhatsApp 或电邮与我联系。

计算器

退休计算器的所有运算都在你的浏览器内完成。只有在你提交表格时，你填写的资料与计算结果才会传送给我们。

What we collect

When you use the retirement calculator or book a consultation, we collect the name, mobile number and email you provide, plus the details you enter in the form (such as age, savings amounts and topics you'd like to discuss).

How we use it

  • To contact you and arrange a consultation;
  • To prepare planning material relevant to your situation;
  • With your consent, to send market updates and financial content.

Storage and sharing

Your details are stored in a Google account privately managed by Kelly Chan and are never sold or rented to third parties. They are shared only where required by law or where necessary to provide the service you asked for.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to stop receiving updates, at any time. Please contact me via WhatsApp or email.

The calculator

All retirement calculator maths runs in your own browser. Your inputs and results are sent to us only when you submit the form.
